Good Morning. Privacii's debut album launch

A New Chapter

After more than a decade refining his craft, Privacii enters a defining new chapter with his debut album, Good Morning, released through Soul Candi Records. The album is a culmination of his evolution as a producer and artist, bringing together his distinctive approach to rhythm, melody and sound design. With Good Morning, Privacii presents a new chapter in an evolving sound shaped by African influence, electronic innovation and over a decade of experience.

Join us on the 12th to hear it with the people who helped make it, and the ones who have been listening from the start.
